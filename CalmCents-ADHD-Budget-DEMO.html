📖 Complete Built-in User Guide

Everything you need to know — designed for quick, overwhelm-free reading.

1

Set Your Monthly Target

Open Dashboard → Edit Budget & Limits, type your monthly income, and tap Auto-Fill 50/30/20 for an instant calm setup.

2

Add Recurring Bills Once

Go to Bills & Subs and add rent, utilities, and subscriptions. They automatically reappear every month so nothing slips through.

3

Check Your Daily Number

Glance at Safe-to-Spend Today on your Dashboard and log daily spending in 3 seconds using the green + Quick Add button.

Your daily number uses one transparent, automatic formula so you never have to do mental math:

Safe-to-Spend Today = (Remaining Budget − Upcoming Unpaid Bills − Reserved Savings) ÷ Remaining Days

If your spending goes above your target, the app shows the real negative daily amount in soft terracotta instead of hiding it at $0 — giving you gentle clarity without shame.

Feeling overwhelmed by charts or numbers? Tap Focus Mode in the top bar. It immediately hides category progress bars and graphs so you only see your Safe-to-Spend Today number and 4 core totals. Your preference is saved automatically.

  • 3-Second Entry: Tap + Quick Add (or the floating + button on mobile), enter the dollar amount, tap an emoji category, and hit Save.
  • Smart Search & Filters: In the Transactions tab, search any note or amount, filter by Expense/Income/Category, or sort by Highest Amount.
  • Accidentally Deleted Something? Every deletion pops up a dark bar at the bottom for 6 seconds with a bright Undo button that restores your entry instantly.
  • Auto-Repeat Every Month: Any bill or subscription marked Recurring Every Month automatically shows up as Unpaid when you switch to a new month.
  • Zero Double-Counting: Ticking a bill's checkbox marks it Paid for that month only, triggers a celebratory confetti burst, and logs one linked expense in Transactions. Unchecking it (or tapping Undo) cleanly removes that linked expense so your Safe-to-Spend balance is never deducted twice.
  • Subscription Audit: At the top of Bills & Subs, see your exact monthly and annual (× 12) subscription cost at a glance.
  • Impulse Pause: Before buying a non-essential item, park it in Impulse & Goals with a 24h, 48h, or 72h timer. Once the urge passes, tap Skipped It! to celebrate with confetti and add that dollar amount to your Impulse Wins Hall of Fame.
  • Multiple Savings Jars: Create separate custom jars (Emergency Fund, Travel, Debt Payoff) with your own emojis and colors, and top them up with 1-tap +$10, +$25, +$50, or ± Custom buttons.
  • Same Browser Rule: Because your data is stored privately inside your browser's local database (without any cloud server), always open this file in the same browser (e.g., Google Chrome) on your device.
  • Device Sync via JSON: To move your budget from laptop to phone (or back up weekly), click Backup JSON above, send the small .json file to your phone, and click Restore JSON.
  • iPhone Tip: On iOS, open the HTML file using Google Chrome or the free Documents by Readdle app for rock-solid offline storage.
